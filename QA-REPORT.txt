GROUP ACTIVITY CREATED
THE ACTUARIAL FIRM DRAFT — “Build your firm with 100 points.”
- Groups of 3 or 4 students.
- One device per group recommended.
- Clear English-only instructions.
- Every student speaks for approximately 2 minutes in the final boardroom presentation.

8 FIXED ROUNDS
1. Choose one target client.
2. Choose exactly two areas of actuarial expertise.
3. Hire exactly two core team profiles.
4. Buy exactly two tools.
5. Choose exactly two free identity traits.
6. Review the firm and budget.
7. Handle a deterministic client alert.
8. Generate the boardroom brief and prepare the oral presentation.

BUDGET / CROSS-DEVICE DETERMINISM
- Starting budget: 100 points.
- Every paid option has one fixed cost stored directly in the HTML data model.
- Budget is always recalculated from the currently selected options. It is NOT incremented/decremented from click history, preventing score drift.
- No Math.random() or random branch is used in the group activity.
- Next-round order is fixed: 1→2→3→4→5→6→7→8.
- The client alert is selected by a fixed rule based on client/expertise:
  • climate client/expertise → Extreme weather shock
  • pensions client/expertise → Longevity assumptions challenged
  • insurance/pricing/reserving → Unexpected claims spike
  • healthcare → Data quality problem
  • otherwise → Board challenges your model
- Same choices therefore produce the same cost, points left, challenge and configuration code on every device.
- Round 3 checks that at least 18 points remain for the minimum valid two-tool combination, preventing a dead-end on the next round.
- Round 4 blocks continuation if the final paid configuration exceeds 100 points.

CLIENT ALERT
