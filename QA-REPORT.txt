AUDIO / PRONUNCIATION PERFORMANCE
Problem found in the deployed code:
- speechSynthesis.getVoices() was called again on every Listen click.
- speechSynthesis.cancel() was called unconditionally on every click.
- Dictionary, Word of the Day and Flashcards pronounced the term plus its whole example sentence.
- Listen & Choose added an unnecessary 180 ms delay before speaking.
- Voice selection did not prefer a local/on-device voice, so a slower remote voice could be selected.

Corrections:
- Voice list is cached at page initialisation.
- One lightweight refresh is allowed at the first real user interaction for browsers that load voices late.
- Local en-GB voices are preferred before remote voices.
- cancel() is used only when speech is actually active or queued.
- Dictionary, Word of the Day and Flashcards pronounce the term only.
- Listen & Choose starts speech immediately; artificial 180 ms delay removed.
- Interrupted speech is restarted safely on Chromium without adding delay to first-click speech.

PWA / UPDATE CACHE
- Service-worker cache version bumped to r4.
- HTML, JS, CSS, vocabulary data and manifest now use network-first behaviour when online, with cache as offline fallback.
- Old cache versions are deleted on activation.
- Service-worker registration uses updateViaCache:'none' and requests an update after registration.
- This reduces the risk of students remaining on an obsolete GitHub Pages version after a deployment.
