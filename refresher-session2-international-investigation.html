☎️ The International Actuarial Hotline

Three offices. One report. Ask questions, collect clues, and present your own 1-minute recap to your English teacher.

Before you start: what is this game?

You are junior actuaries at Northstar Insurance. Management needs a risk report tomorrow, but the figures do not agree. London changed a pricing assumption, Dublin is checking unusual data, and Toronto is waiting for confirmation.

Your mission: Call the three offices, choose useful questions, collect evidence, then decide whether the report is ready.

During the game: Everyone writes one recap sentence after each call. At the end, each student presents a one-minute oral recap directly to the English teacher.

📒 Your live mission notes

Each student writes one note after every call to prepare their individual 1-minute oral presentation to the teacher.
