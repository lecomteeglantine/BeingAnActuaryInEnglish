🔎 Listening strategy for assessment day

Before listening: skim the questions and identify what type of information is needed; do not try to memorise every word. First viewing: identify the central message and the speaker’s purpose. Second viewing: listen for quantities, contrast words, examples and cause-effect links. After listening: distinguish what was actually stated from what you inferred; support True/False decisions with evidence when asked.

Different tasks require different answers: choose one option for an MCQ, a brief fact for a short answer, the complete sequence for a mechanism, and a short explanation in your own words for an interpretation.
