M2 SESSION 1 GROUP ACTIVITY — R32
The Global Actuarial Crisis Room
- Team selector: 3 or 4 players.
- Four international missions: UK pensions, US health insurance, Canada pricing, Australia/New Zealand catastrophe risk.
- 16 fixed decisions: Risk, Decision, Tact, Justification.
- Fixed scoring: 32 points total, no randomness.
- Same choice on any device = same score, same next options and same feedback.
- Optional WebAudio sound effects and speechSynthesis client messages.
- Team Recap Builder visible throughout the game.
- Final recap generated from the team's actual choices.
- Final oral task: 2 minutes maximum for the whole team; explain and justify key choices.
- Diplomatic language integrates passive/hedging/modals/less certain language/clarification strategies from the supplied class materials.
