The Global Actuarial Crisis Room

Work in a team of 3 or 4. Advise four international clients, make actuarial decisions, communicate difficult information tactfully, and build your final recap as you play.

  • Choose 3 or 4 players; speaking roles adapt automatically.
  • Complete 4 English-speaking-world client missions.
  • Fixed deterministic scoring: the same answer always gives the same points.
  • Same choices on different devices lead to the same options and outcomes.
  • Realistic illustrations and optional sound throughout the activity.
  • Finish with one 2-minute maximum team recap: explain and justify your most important choices.
