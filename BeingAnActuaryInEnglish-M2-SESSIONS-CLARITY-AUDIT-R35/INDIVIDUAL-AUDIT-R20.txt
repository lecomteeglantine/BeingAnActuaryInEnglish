R20 individual functionality audit

Fixes in this release:
- Prevents accidental double activation of mission-clear transitions.
- Prevents rapid double-clicks from skipping Client Meeting questions.
- Debounces Risk Lab tile toggles and explains when the five-slot tray is full.
- Adds explicit feedback when clicking a Brief/Boardroom destination before selecting a source item.
- Handles browsers without speech synthesis by disabling Listen buttons with a clear fallback message.
- Migrates R17/R18/R19 session progress into the R20 storage key instead of wiping progress.
- Sanitises persisted Client Meeting error text.
- Adds robust modal aria/inert state and closes audio/modal state on page hide.
- Adds type=button to dynamically generated action buttons.
- Keeps M1 visibly highlighted in the navigation.
- Service-worker cache bumped to R20 and references the R20 JS/CSS files.
