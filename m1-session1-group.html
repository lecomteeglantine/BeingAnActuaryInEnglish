M1 · SESSION 1 · GROUP ACTIVITY

Build the Ideal Actuarial Firm

Work in a team of 3 or 4 students. Imagine that you are founding a new actuarial consultancy. Your goal is to build a firm that is useful, credible, ethical and easy to explain to a potential client.

← Session 1
01

BEFORE YOU START

Set up your team

  1. Make a team of 3 or 4.
  2. Use one device for the whole group. One student controls the screen; everyone must take part in every decision.
  3. Speak English throughout the activity. Use the language support on the page whenever you need it.
  4. Choose a temporary project manager. Their job is to keep the group moving, not to make all the decisions.
Final task: pitch your ideal actuarial firm to the class. Every student must speak for approximately 2 minutes.

USEFUL LANGUAGE

Our firm specialises in…
Our clients need us to…
The main risk is…
We would use data and models to…
What makes our firm different is…
1

IDENTITY

Name your firm

Choose a professional name and write one short sentence that explains what your firm wants to achieve.

2

SERVICES

Choose what your firm does

Select exactly three services. Be ready to explain why they belong together.

3

CLIENT

Choose your first major client

Select one client. Discuss what they are worried about and what an actuary could do for them.

4

TEAM

Design the team

Assign one responsibility to each student. If you are a group of 3, combine two roles.

Lead actuaryCoordinates the analysis and final recommendation.
Data & modelling leadChecks data and builds or challenges models.
Risk & ethics leadChallenges assumptions and identifies limitations.
Client communication leadTurns technical results into clear advice.
5

VALUES

Decide what makes your firm worth trusting

Select three values. For each one, prepare a concrete example of what it would look like in practice.

6

FINAL DELIVERABLE

Prepare your pitch

Your presentation should make the class understand both what actuaries do and why your firm would be useful.

Student 1 · The firmName, mission and the problem you want to solve.
Student 2 · The actuarial workServices, data, models, risk and uncertainty.
Student 3 · The clientYour first client, their risks and your recommendations.
Student 4 · Why trust you?Team, values and what makes the firm different.

3-person group? Combine Students 3 and 4. Each student still speaks for approximately 2 minutes.
