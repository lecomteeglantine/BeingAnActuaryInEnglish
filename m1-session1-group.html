READ THIS FIRST

Your mission

  1. Work in a group of 3 or 4 students.
  2. Use one device per group if possible. Discuss every decision before clicking.
  3. You start with 100 points. Every client, expertise, team member and tool has a fixed cost.
  4. You must stay within budget. You cannot choose everything.
  5. There is no random scoring: the same choices always give the same cost, the same next round and the same final challenge on every device.
  6. At the end, prepare a boardroom presentation. Every student speaks for approximately 2 minutes.
English only: explain your choices to your teammates before you validate them. The language and pronunciation support on the page is there to help you.

ROUND 1 · CHOOSE ONE

Who will your firm work for?

Choose your first target market. This decision will influence the challenge your firm receives later.

Discuss before you click: What risks would this client expect an actuary to understand?

ROUND 2 · CHOOSE EXACTLY TWO

What will your firm specialise in?

You need a clear offer. Choose two areas of expertise that make sense together.

Discuss: Why do these two areas belong in the same firm?

ROUND 3 · HIRE EXACTLY TWO

Build your core team

Your founders cannot do everything. Buy two additional profiles for your new firm.

Discuss: What can your chosen people do that the other profiles cannot?

ROUND 4 · CHOOSE EXACTLY TWO

Invest in your tools

Technology costs money. Choose the two tools your team really needs.

Budget warning: expensive is not automatically better. Your choices must fit your client and expertise.

ROUND 5 · FREE CHOICE · CHOOSE EXACTLY TWO

What kind of firm are you building?

These identity choices cost 0 points, but you must be able to prove them in your final presentation.

Discuss: What concrete behaviour would make a client believe these two words?

ROUND 6 · BUDGET CHECK

Does your firm make sense?

Before you continue, discuss these three questions:

  1. Which choice gives your firm its strongest advantage?
  2. What did you not buy because of the budget?
  3. Where is your firm potentially weak?

ROUND 7 · CLIENT ALERT

Your first real test

Important: your challenge is generated by a fixed rule from your client and expertise. It is not random.

FINAL ROUND · BOARDROOM

Turn your draft into a firm

You have made the technical choices. Now make the firm memorable and prepare to defend it.

YOUR ORAL PRESENTATION

Every student speaks for approximately 2 minutes.

Student 3 · How you built the firmYour team, tools, budget choices and trade-offs.

3-person group? Student 3 also explains the client alert response. Everyone still speaks for approximately 2 minutes.
