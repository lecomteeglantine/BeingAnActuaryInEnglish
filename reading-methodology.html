READING EXAM TRAINER · B2

Reading Methodology

Learn how to understand unfamiliar actuarial texts and answer exam questions accurately.

Your strategy: understand the question, locate evidence, check the exact meaning, then choose your answer. You do not need to translate the whole article. The seven-minute version of this method is at the bottom of the page.

Exam-day checklist

Before submitting your answers, check:

  • Have I identified the article’s main idea rather than a minor detail?
  • Did I read every question carefully, including words like NOT, mainly and TWO?
  • For every True/False answer, can I point to the supporting paragraph?
  • Did I infer vocabulary from surrounding sentences, not guess from a familiar-looking word?
  • Did I reject answers that use absolute claims not supported by the text?
  • Did I select exactly two distinct ideas for each final inference question?

Suggested reading plan: 2–3 minutes to map the text; answer factual questions; then True/False with evidence; vocabulary; inference; and a final check. Adjust this according to the time your teacher allows.
