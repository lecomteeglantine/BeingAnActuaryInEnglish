YOUR MISSION

Get the station back on air.

  1. Listen first. Every challenge is built around real audio files — no reading aloud and no microphone access.
  2. Repair six studio systems. Lost files, stress, calls, interference, adverts and the final live countdown.
  3. Protect your three studio fuses. Wrong answers cost a fuse, but emergency backup power prevents a dead end.
  4. Use headphones. British-English pronunciation, word stress and listening discrimination are the whole point.
