MINI SPEAKING TASK

Use the English, not just the maths

Prepare a two-minute mini-pitch: one finding, one business consequence, one recommendation, one caveat.

1 · PrepareChoose the evidence you need.
2 · StructureLead with the main message.
3 · SpeakUse full sentences and signposting.
4 · ChallengeAnswer one follow-up question.

USEFUL LANGUAGE

Professional sentence starters

  • “The main point to take away is…”
  • “Compared with the previous scenario…”
  • “The data suggest that…, although…”
  • “One limitation we need to keep in mind is…”
  • “On this basis, I would recommend…”
