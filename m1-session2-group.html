The Actuarial Museum

Four exhibits. Two minutes. One story.

Your team has been hired to curate a new museum exhibition about the history of actuarial science. Work on one device per team. In each of four galleries, agree on four decisions, then prepare one short speaking note. At the end, use those four notes for one team pitch of 2 minutes maximum in total.

YOUR MISSION

Do not just collect facts. Build a story.

  1. Choose your team. Select 3 or 4 students. The questions and score are identical; only the roles and speaking split change. Each role is linked to a specific part of the final pitch.
  2. Check the audio. If Sound: ON is displayed, click Test sound: you should hear a short museum chime. Sound controls the game effects and the optional curator voice.
  3. Complete each gallery. Discuss all four decisions and select exactly one answer for each.
  4. Build the pitch as you play. Before saving a gallery, review the suggested note, edit it if needed, and keep one 30–40 word note: exhibit → evidence → why it matters → link to the story. The game shows which speaker owns that section.
  5. Save before moving on. A gallery is only validated after you click Save gallery. If you later change an answer or pitch note, re-save it.
  6. Final deliverable. Give ONE pitch of 2:00 maximum for the whole team, not 2 minutes per student. Every student must speak.
  7. Tell a connected story. Present Galleries I–IV in order and finish by explaining what this history tells us about the origins of actuarial science.
