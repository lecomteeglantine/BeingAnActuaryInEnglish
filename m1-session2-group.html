The Actuarial Museum

Four exhibits. Two minutes. One story.

Your team has been hired to curate a new museum exhibition about the history of actuarial science. You only have room for four major exhibits. Choose carefully, justify every decision, and build your final two-minute pitch as you go.

👥 3 or 4 students🏛️ 4 galleries🎤 2-minute team pitch💾 Auto-save

YOUR MISSION

Do not just collect facts. Build a story.

  1. Debate. Read all options aloud and agree on one answer as a team.
  2. Curate. Every choice shapes your exhibition and your score.
  3. Prepare your pitch. After each gallery, keep or edit the suggested sentence in your Pitch Notebook.
  4. Present. At the end, every student speaks. The whole team has a maximum of two minutes.
