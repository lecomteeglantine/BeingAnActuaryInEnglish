SESSION 1 GAME — TESTED LOGIC
PASS
- 3-player and 4-player role sets are distinct, but mission data and scoring are identical.
- Four fixed missions: UK, USA, Canada, Australia/New Zealand.
- Four fixed decisions per mission: Risk, Decision, Tact, Justification.
- No Math.random() in the game.
- Maximum score is deterministically 32 for both 3-player and 4-player teams.
- Independent state simulations with identical answers produce identical answer state and score.
- Save/reload sanitisation preserves valid progress and score.
- Invalid/gapped saved states are repaired rather than allowing later steps to survive an earlier missing answer.
- A mission is not treated as completed until “Save mission recap” has actually been used.
- Final recap is generated directly from stored choices, so changing a choice changes the recap deterministically.

BUG FIXED DURING R37 AUDIT — OLD-DECISION EDITING
- Reviewing an earlier mission could previously leave later completion flags in a non-sequential state during the same browser session.
- R37 now invalidates the reviewed mission AND all later mission-completion flags whenever an old decision is changed.
- Later recorded answers are not randomly regenerated; the team can reconfirm them in order.
- This prevents progression holes and guarantees that the final recap has been explicitly revalidated after an upstream change.

DOUBLE-CLICK PROTECTION
PASS / HARDENED
- Mission-transition actions use one global navigation lock.
- Lock duration increased to 360 ms.
- The lock survives DOM re-rendering, so a rapid second click on a newly rendered mission button is ignored.
- During the lock, mission navigation buttons have pointer-events disabled and the game mount exposes aria-busy="true".
- An execution test confirmed: two immediate transition calls execute once; a later click after the lock executes normally.
